# Edge Delta

> AI-powered observability platform with intelligent telemetry pipelines and agentic AI teammates for SRE, security, and DevOps teams.

Edge Delta is an observability platform built for the AI era. It combines intelligent telemetry pipelines with AI Teammates that continuously analyze real-time production data to automate root cause analysis, reduce alert fatigue, and surface actionable signals from noise.

## Product

- [AI Teammates](https://edgedelta.com/product/agentic-teammates): Agentic observability team that continuously analyzes real-time production data to automate root cause analysis and surface actionable signals from noise.
- [Telemetry Pipelines](https://edgedelta.com/product/telemetry-pipelines): Intelligent foundation for controlling and routing logs, metrics, traces, and events from any source to any destination.
- [Anomaly Detection](https://edgedelta.com/product/anomaly-detection): Detect and resolve incidents faster with correlated context across all services.
- [Security Data Pipelines](https://edgedelta.com/product/security-data-pipelines): Control and visibility over security data with real-time standardization, enrichment, and streaming.
- [Logs](https://edgedelta.com/product/logs): Process, transform, and enrich every log at the edge or in the cloud.
- [Metrics](https://edgedelta.com/product/metrics): Analyze metrics end-to-end to improve response times, lower costs, and mute noise.
- [Traces](https://edgedelta.com/product/traces): Deep visibility and full command over trace data with advanced tracing capabilities.
- [Integrations](https://edgedelta.com/product/integrations): Seamless integrations to connect with your existing tools and streamline workflows.
- [Product Overview](https://edgedelta.com/product): Overview of the Edge Delta platform capabilities.

## AI Teammates

- [SRE Teammate](https://edgedelta.com/product/agentic-teammates/sre): AI-powered SRE that automates incident triage, root cause analysis, and remediation.
- [Security Engineer Teammate](https://edgedelta.com/product/agentic-teammates/security-engineer): AI teammate for automated security monitoring and threat investigation.
- [Software Engineer Teammate](https://edgedelta.com/product/agentic-teammates/software-engineer): AI teammate that explores codebases, reviews pull requests, fixes bugs, and opens PRs, with read-only-by-default and approval-gated writes.
- [Issue Coordinator Teammate](https://edgedelta.com/product/agentic-teammates/issue-coordinator): AI teammate for coordinating and managing issues across teams.
